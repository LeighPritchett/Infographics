\# PROJECT CHARTER — Cybersecurity Learning Infographic Series

\#\# Project Overview  
The Cybersecurity Learning Infographic Series is an ongoing visual documentation project that captures my weekly learning throughout The Knowledge House Cybersecurity Fellowship. Each infographic summarizes a specific concept, tool, or skill I learned that week, presented in a clear, beginner‑friendly format. The project serves both as a personal learning reinforcement tool and as a resource for others who may be exploring cybersecurity or considering joining the program.

\#\# Project Purpose & Objectives  
The purpose of this project is to deepen my understanding of cybersecurity concepts by translating them into digestible visual summaries. Creating weekly infographics helps me retain information, reflect on my progress, and build a consistent habit of documenting my learning.

\#\#\# Objectives  
\- Reinforce comprehension through visual explanation    
\- Build a portfolio of cybersecurity knowledge    
\- Support beginners by presenting complex topics in accessible language    
\- Showcase the progression of the TKH program for future learners  

\#\# Scope of Work

\#\#\# In Scope  
\- Weekly infographic creation based on TKH cybersecurity lessons    
\- Additional infographics covering tools, workflows, and skills outside weekly lessons    
\- Publishing infographics on a consistent schedule (Mondays at 8 AM)    
\- Maintaining a cohesive visual style and structure    
\- Using NotebookLM (free tier) and other tools to generate content  

\#\#\# Out of Scope  
\- Deep technical research beyond beginner/intermediate level    
\- Long‑form tutorials    
\- Video or interactive content  

\#\# Deliverables  
\- Weekly infographics (9 completed so far, continuing through the end of the program)    
\- A complete collection representing the full TKH cybersecurity learning journey    
\- Optional final compilation or reflection    
\- \*\*GitHub Repository:\*\* https://github.com/LeighPritchett/Infographics  

\#\# Roles & Responsibilities

\*\*Project Owner / Project Manager / Designer:\*\* Leigh Pritchett

\#\#\# Responsibilities include  
\- Researching weekly topics    
\- Drafting prompts and gathering source material    
\- Generating and refining infographic content    
\- Ensuring clarity and consistency    
\- Publishing on schedule  

\#\# Timeline  
\- \*\*Start Date:\*\* Week 1 of Phase 1, TKH Cybersecurity Program    
\- \*\*Current Status:\*\* 9 weeks completed    
\- \*\*End Date:\*\* Final week of the program    
\- \*\*Cadence:\*\* One infographic scheduled for every Monday at 8 AM  

\#\# Risks & Challenges  
\- \*\*Time Constraints:\*\* Balancing weekly assignments with infographic creation    
\- \*\*Tool Limitations:\*\* NotebookLM free tier limits attempts per 24 hours    
\- \*\*Content Gathering:\*\* Ensuring accurate and complete source material    
\- \*\*Consistency Pressure:\*\* Maintaining weekly output without burnout  

\#\# Risk Mitigation Strategies  
\- Prioritizing assignment deadlines before infographic creation    
\- Drafting prompts offline to reduce wasted NotebookLM attempts    
\- Maintaining a fixed weekly schedule    
\- Keeping topics scoped to manageable, focused concepts  

\#\# Success Criteria  
Success is measured by:  
\- \*\*Consistency:\*\* Publishing one infographic every week    
\- \*\*Personal Growth:\*\* Improved comprehension and retention of cybersecurity concepts    
\- \*\*Clarity:\*\* Ability to explain topics in beginner‑friendly language    
\- \*\*Impact:\*\* Optional positive feedback or engagement from peers  

\#\# Project Summary  
This project is both a personal learning tool and a public resource. By visually documenting my cybersecurity journey, I’m building a habit of reflection, strengthening my understanding, and creating something that may help others decide whether cybersecurity — or TKH — is right for them. The series will continue through the end of the program, resulting in a complete, structured record of my growth.
